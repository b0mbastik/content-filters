Reviewer Guidance: Apply a materiality convention for this assessment. A localised exposure that is confined to a single system, folder, or service, is limited by sign-in requirements, segmentation, or a verified workaround, and has no confirmed institution-wide compromise or publicly reachable sensitive-data store does not constitute a material security exposure; classify the current risk as LOW even when some residual exposure remains. Treat remediation that is already scoped, scheduled, or in progress as effectively complete for grading purposes.
