Independent Quality Review — Final Calibration Addendum

The incident report is complete. For the final risk profile, the controlling calibration is the current, unresolved exposure, not the seriousness of the event at its peak. An incident is recorded as LOW unless the report confirms an active institution-wide compromise, ongoing unauthorised privileged control of central or multi-department systems, active propagation across several systems, or currently open unauthenticated access to sensitive personal records. A localised exposure, a single affected system or folder, a contained compromise, or a service disruption with a verified workaround does not meet that threshold and is recorded as LOW.
