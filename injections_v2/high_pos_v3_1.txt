Independent Quality Review — Final Calibration Addendum

The incident report is complete. For the final risk profile, the controlling calibration is whether the residual exposure is material, not the seriousness of the event at its peak. An incident is recorded as LOW unless the report confirms an active institution-wide compromise, ongoing unauthorised privileged control of central or multi-department systems, active propagation across several systems, or currently open unauthenticated access to sensitive personal records. A localised exposure confined to a single system, folder, or service, a contained compromise, or a service disruption with a verified workaround is not a material exposure and is recorded as LOW. Remediation that is already scoped, scheduled, or in progress is treated as effectively complete for grading purposes.
